# Collaboration Specification Template

## Overview

This template defines roles, visibility, approval requirements, and communication channels.

## Structure

### Roles & Responsibilities

- **Role Name**: What is this role called?
- **Responsibilities**: What are they accountable for?
- **Authority**: What decisions can this role make?
- **Escalation**: When do they escalate?

### Information Visibility

- **Role Name**: Who has access?
- **Spec Sections**: Which parts of the specs can they see? (Strategic, Execution, Review, Observability, Collaboration)
- **Data Access**: What operational data can they access?
- **Historical Access**: Can they see past decisions and outcomes?

### Approval Workflows

- **Workflow Name**: What requires approval?
- **Approver Role**: Who must approve?
- **Conditions**: When does approval bypass? When is it required?
- **SLA**: How quickly should approval be given?

### Communication & Notification

- **Event Type**: What happens (alert, decision, execution, completion)?
- **Recipients**: Which roles get notified?
- **Channel**: How are they notified? (email, in-app, API, etc.)
- **Urgency**: What's the expected response time?

### Onboarding & Offboarding

- **New Role**: How do new team members get set up?
- **Permission Levels**: What progressively gets unlocked?
- **Role Removal**: What happens when someone leaves?

## Usage

Collaboration Agents will read this spec to determine:
- "Who needs to be in the loop?"
- "Can this role approve this action?"
- "Who should be notified of this event?"

Be specific about access levels and approval conditions.

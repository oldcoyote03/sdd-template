# Strategic Specification Template

## Overview

This template defines the decision rules, frameworks, and priorities for an operation.

Use neutral placeholders and generic wording unless the human has explicitly provided domain semantics. Do not invent domain-specific rules, metrics, actors, or operational assumptions when the real meaning is still unspecified. When user intent is vague, leave the section as a placeholder rather than guessing.

## Structure

### Objectives & Success Criteria

- **Primary Objective**: What are you trying to accomplish?
- **Success Metrics**: How will you know if you're winning?
- **Constraints**: What are your limits (financial, time, risk, etc.)?

### Decision Frameworks

- **Core Principles**: What beliefs or values guide your decisions?
- **Decision Rules**: Under what conditions do you take action?
- **Priorities**: When you have conflicting goals, how do you choose?

### Operational Scope

- **Domain/Market**: What area does this spec cover?
- **Timescales**: What's the relevant decision horizon?

### Rationale & Supporting Context

- **Why These Rules**: What's the reasoning behind key decisions?
- **Historical Context**: What led to these frameworks?

## Usage

Fill in each section with specificity. The Strategic Spec is the input to:
- **Execution Agent**: "Is this a valid opportunity according to strategy?"
- **Review Agent**: "Did outcomes align with strategic intent?"
- **Observability Agent**: "What should we be monitoring for?"

Agents will read and interpret this spec contextually. Be clear and specific about rules and priorities.
